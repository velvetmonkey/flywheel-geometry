# Heat pump installation notes

Daikin Altherma 3 R, 9 kW model, replacing the old electric resistive system. Installed by Brisbane Climate Solutions, $14,200 after rebate.

**Sizing decision**: 9 kW vs 11 kW was the main fork. 11 kW handles peak demand on the coldest mornings and has marginal headroom; 9 kW is sized closer to average demand and cycles more on cold mornings. Took 9 kW because the south-facing wall gets afternoon sun and the average heating load is well below the peak. Cycling matters less than oversize-on-mild-days does for COP.

**COP measurements** (logged from inverter output):
- Mild morning (15°C ambient): 4.1
- Cool morning (8°C): 3.4
- Cold morning (3°C, rare in Brisbane): 2.6
- Average over winter: ~3.6

That's a factor-of-3.6 multiplier on grid energy. Combined with the solar feed and overnight low tariffs, the effective heating cost per kWh of delivered heat is around 5¢. Not free, but close.

**Anomalies**:
- Defrost cycle is more frequent than I'd expected on humid mornings. Datasheet predicted maybe 2 cycles/hour at the design point; I'm seeing 3-4. Working theory: Brisbane humidity puts more frost on the outdoor coil than the European testing standard captures. Talking to the installer about whether we want to adjust the defrost trigger.
- Compressor noise is audible from the bedroom on the lowest fan setting. Acoustic blanket over the unit reduced it by ~5 dB — doable.

**The economics**: payback period is about 6 years against the resistive baseline. Lower if energy prices keep climbing; lower still if we monetise the carbon offset (we're not currently). Ten-year NPV is clearly positive even with conservative assumptions.

**Underlying point**: heat pumps work because they move heat rather than create it. The COP is a kind of "leverage ratio" on grid energy. Same idea as carry trades in finance — a good carry trade isn't generating returns from nothing, it's leveraging an existing structural rate differential. Both produce value through transport rather than creation. Both have failure modes (low ambient temp / regime change) that erode the leverage suddenly.
