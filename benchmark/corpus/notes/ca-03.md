# Negotiation prep — equity vs cash

Annual review prep for late June. Working through what I want to ask for and what I'd accept.

**Current package**: base + bonus structure with a 70/30 cash split. Vested equity in Meridian's GP — small but real exposure to firm-level performance. Total comp last year ~$X (target).

**What I want for next cycle**:
1. **Higher equity allocation, lower cash**. The cash is more than I need at the current marginal-tax rate. Marginal $1 of equity has more long-run expected value than marginal $1 of cash, given my belief Meridian is structurally well-positioned for the next 5 years. Specifically: ask for 50/50 instead of 70/30.
2. **Carve-out for my external research**. Want explicit written permission to publish work outside Meridian's IP umbrella, on the geodesic-retrieval line specifically. Not asking for time off; asking for non-encumbrance.
3. **Sabbatical option vested at year 12**. Currently no formal sabbatical. Would accept a $X reduction in cash comp in exchange for a guaranteed 3-month sabbatical in 2028.

**The firm's likely concerns**:
- (1) is fine — Meridian wants partners with skin in the game, not arbitrageable employees. They'll counter on the *delta* (50/50 too aggressive; 60/40 likely).
- (2) is the sticky one. IP umbrella is broad and the firm has had problems with employees publishing things that could be argued to use firm-developed signals. I'll need to specifically describe the scope (interpretability research, not investment research) and accept a review process.
- (3) might surprise them but isn't unreasonable for a 12-year tenure. Frame as retention; don't apologise for it.

**My BATNA**: I'd be employable elsewhere at similar comp without much friction. Goldman Sachs Asset Management's systematic team has been recruiting; so has Citadel. The walk-away rate isn't bluff — it's just expensive enough that I won't trigger it lightly.

**The ask I keep almost forgetting**: better deferred-compensation structure. Currently the bonus is paid in cash with a partial RSU lock. The lock structure is generous but the *timing* of cash flow doesn't suit my life — I'd rather defer more, take more in equity, less liquidity now. This is essentially free for the firm and meaningful to me.

**Negotiation discipline**: do not anchor on dollar amounts in the first conversation. Anchor on *structure* (equity %, IP carve-out, sabbatical). The dollar amounts follow from the structure. Anchoring on dollars first is how junior negotiations go badly — you get a number that fits the existing structure rather than the structure you wanted.

**Note for next year**: write this kind of memo *before* the meeting, not after, every year. Last year I went in with vibes and got vibes-quality outcomes.
