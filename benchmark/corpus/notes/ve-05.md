# Electric mountain bike — maintenance schedule

Trek Powerfly FS 9 LT, bought 2023. 2,400 km. Mostly fire trails on the D'Aguilar Range, occasional weekend trips up to Mount Mee.

**The reality of e-bike maintenance**: the drivetrain takes far more abuse per km than an analogue MTB because the motor adds torque the chain wasn't designed for. Chain wear is roughly 1.7× faster on the e-bike than on my analogue. Cassettes the same. Means budgeting drivetrain replacement every 8-9 months instead of yearly.

**Routine schedule**:
- Every ride: chain wipe + lube reapplication. Skipping this once turns into a stiff drivetrain quickly.
- Weekly: brake check, tire pressure, quick visual on cables and headset.
- Monthly: torque check on key bolts (saddle, stem, brake levers, motor mounts). The Bosch motor unit has specific bolt torque specs that drift if not checked.
- Quarterly: bottom bracket service, suspension fork lower-leg service, drivetrain measurement (chain stretch gauge — replace at 0.5%).
- Annual: full service at a Trek-authorised shop. Roughly $400. Includes battery diagnostic, motor firmware update, full bearing inspection.

**Battery**: 750 Wh internal. Currently shows 92% state-of-health on the Bosch eBike Connect app after 2 years and roughly 350 charge cycles. Linear-projecting, that's another 4-5 years before I'd consider replacement. The replacement cost ($1,400) is the major non-routine spend on the horizon.

**Pattern**: the calendar cadence isn't doing the work. The kilometre cadence is. A ride through wet leaf-litter in spring eats more drivetrain than three dry-fire-trail rides — but the maintenance list doesn't notice that. Smart operators (Marcus does this with his) substitute condition-based maintenance for calendar-based: chain stretch, brake pad thickness, tyre tread depth, all measured directly. Replace when the measurement crosses threshold, not when the calendar says so.

**Same shape as everything else**: condition-based monitoring beats schedule-based monitoring for any system where the wear rate is non-stationary. Solar inverters, heat pumps, factor models, horses. The schedule is a coarse approximation; the measurement is the truth.
