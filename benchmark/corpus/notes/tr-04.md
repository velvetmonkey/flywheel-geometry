# Bali workation — venue research

Spent an evening on this. Considering 3-4 weeks in Bali in early 2027 to write the long-form piece on geodesic retrieval methods. Looking for a venue with reliable internet, quiet workspace, decent climate, and ideally riding nearby (asking for a lot from one location).

**Constraints**:
- Internet: must support reliable video calls with Brisbane (3-hour offset, manageable). Past Bali trips the connectivity was patchy. Need to verify each venue, not trust the website.
- Climate: rainy season Nov-March; aim for April or pre-rains in October. Highlands cooler than the south.
- Workspace: a real desk with a real chair. Not a cushioned daybed marketed as a "co-working space." Real keyboard ergonomics matter on a 4-week stay.
- Riding: stretch goal. There are stables near Ubud but the local breed isn't ideal for dressage work — would mostly be trail riding. Acceptable as recreation, not training.

**Shortlist**:
1. **Outpost Ubud** — the well-known co-living space. Pros: predictable internet, workspace built for this use case. Cons: 30+ other people on calls in the same building means the soundscape is bad in peak hours; community vibe is forced; not suitable if I need real focus.
2. **Selo Sani Estate** (north of Ubud, in Tegallalang). Pros: private villa with good desk setup, on-site fibre, quiet. Cons: more isolated; need to drive into town for groceries. Estimated $3,200 / month.
3. **Sayan Eco-Lodge** (Sayan ridge, west of Ubud). Pros: spectacular setting, reliable Starlink, on-site chef. Cons: most expensive option, $5,800 / month.

**Decision logic**: option 2 wins on price/value if the internet checks out. Going to ask the owner for a 24-hour speedtest log before committing. A bad connection at 3am Brisbane time would kill calls; need the histogram, not just peak speed.

**The bigger question**: is the workation actually the right shape for the writing? Robin pointed out that I tend to over-optimise for "cool environment" and under-optimise for "writing constraints." The same writing might happen faster and better at the desk in our spare bedroom in Brisbane. The Bali version is more about *experience* than productivity. Worth being honest about that. Doesn't disqualify the trip, but reframes what it's for.

**Decision**: probably go, but call it a sabbatical not a workation. Lower the writing-output expectation; raise the rest-and-recovery expectation. Different metrics for whether it succeeded.
