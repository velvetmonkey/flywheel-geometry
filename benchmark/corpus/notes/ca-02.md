# Mentor sessions with Marcus — 2026 Q1

Quarterly check-in with Marcus over a long lunch on the Sunshine Coast. Capturing the threads while fresh.

**On the geodesic-retrieval project**: he's sceptical of the framing but interested in the falsifier. Specifically pushed back on "the geometry isn't in the network, it's in the world" — calls it overreach. His version: "What you're testing is whether activation-derived neighbourhoods are operationally more useful than embedding-derived neighbourhoods. That's a worthy question. Don't dress it up as cosmology." Fair. Adjusted my own framing as a result.

**On the Meridian fork**: he's been through the equivalent (left a similar shop at 38 to do something more open-ended; has visible regret about *some* of how that went, but not the choice itself). His specific advice: "Don't optimise for which path is better. Optimise for which one you can stay curious about for ten years. The curiosity dominates the comp differential at any reasonable horizon." Same shape as his earlier "which version of you do you want to be at 60" framing.

**On Caspian / riding**: Marcus rides too — used to event seriously, now mostly trail. Spent fifteen minutes on the geometry of the half-pass, which he thinks I've been over-thinking. His view: stop trying to *engineer* the lateral aids; let the rhythm carry the movement. Actionable; took it to Priya, who agreed.

**Marcus's frame I keep stealing**: "The world has fewer experts than you think and the experts have less to teach you than you think. The thing that compounds is your own attention to the work — *how* you look at it, not *who* tells you how to look at it." Said in the context of why he stopped going to industry conferences. Will probably feel different to me at his age.
