# Interpretability fellowship application — draft notes

Drafting the statement of purpose. Three things I want to communicate:

**1. What I'd build, given a fellowship slot.** A retrieval system over a personal knowledge base that uses activation-derived geometry rather than text embeddings. The hypothesis is that cross-domain conceptual bridges live in the curved structure of model activations and are systematically lost by cosine retrieval over flat embedding vectors. Falsifiable in a week with a small benchmark.

**2. Why this isn't just a feature.** If the hypothesis lands, it's a primitive — a portable representation of conceptual position usable across any knowledge tool, not just my notes. If it doesn't land, the empirical falsifier is published anyway and the field knows one more thing.

**3. What I'd need.** A modest GPU budget for activation extraction across a few open-source models, an academic advisor familiar with the recent geometry-zoo work, and time. The corpus and queries are already in shape.

The risk of this kind of project — Marcus pointed it out at lunch — is the seduction of the framing. "Geometry of meaning" is a beautiful phrase that's also done a lot of philosophical work for projects that didn't have measurement underneath. I need to lead with the falsifier, not the framing. Describe the bench first, the philosophy second.

What I keep coming back to: the strongest version of any speculative project is the one where you can describe the experiment that would refute it and you'd actually update on the result. If I can't articulate that, I haven't earned the fellowship.
