# Solar inverter telemetry — anomaly check

Fronius Symo 8.2 inverter, 9.6 kW north-facing array. Telemetry exposed via Fronius Solar.web with a local Modbus TCP feed I tap into a Home Assistant dashboard.

**Today's anomaly**: noticed strings 1 and 2 diverging by ~12% in expected output around midday yesterday. String 1 outperforming. Cleared today. Either temporary shading on string 2 (likely — a building-site crane went up across the street last week and casts a transient shadow), or a connection issue starting to develop.

**Diagnostic chain**:
1. Pull last 48h of per-string output → confirm the divergence pattern matches sun angle and the new shadow window.
2. If shadow-correlated, no action; just notes for the future. If shadow-uncorrelated, escalate to electrical inspection.
3. Right now the data weakly supports shadow. Will check again on a fully clear day.

**Underlying point**: the value of long-running telemetry is *baseline*. Without two years of normal data I'd have no way to distinguish "this is the new normal" from "this is the start of degradation." The Modbus capture is mundane plumbing that lets me detect changes I'd otherwise rationalise away.

**Missing data problem**: the local capture went down for about a week last December (Home Assistant container issue). That hole is annoying because it's exactly the holiday period — high consumption baseline interrupted. I have to either impute it or treat that period as masked. Default to masked for any year-on-year comparisons.

**Pattern across instruments**: solar telemetry, the Polestar's telemetry, the heat pump's COP logs, my own riding session videos. They're all the same shape — long-running streams with low base-rate of true anomalies, where the value comes from baseline-conditioned change detection rather than absolute thresholds. The instruments are different; the diagnostic discipline is identical.

**Connection to risk modelling**: the desk's regime-conditional models work the same way. There's no absolute "danger threshold" for VaR; there's a baseline, and there are deviations from baseline that need investigating. Either you have the baseline or you don't. The investment in capture infrastructure is the same on Symo telemetry as it is on factor returns — boring, ongoing, irreplaceable.
