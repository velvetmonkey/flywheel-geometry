# Trailer loading reset training plan

Caspian had a bad experience at a clinic in February — got off-balance during a hill descent and now associates the trailer with the moment of fear. He'll load if pushed but only with significant resistance. Need to reset the association before our next event in June.

**Diagnosis**: not a training problem; an emotional learning problem. The mechanics of loading are fine. The aversion is in the anticipated trajectory, not the act.

**Reset protocol** (drawn from Warwick Schiller's approach, adapted):

1. **Week 1**: trailer parked in the arena, doors open, no expectation of loading. Walk past it during normal sessions. Let him sniff. No food bribery — that creates a different conditioning loop and confuses the signal.
2. **Week 2**: invite approach without insisting. Hold him on the lead, walk *near* the ramp, walk away if he looks tense, return when he relaxes. The repetition of "near, then leaving" decouples proximity from threat.
3. **Week 3**: invite a single foot on the ramp. Reward by walking off, not by pushing further. Repeat until he offers two feet, three, four. The progression is *his* not mine.
4. **Week 4**: full loading, but treat the inside of the trailer as a place to hang out, not a corridor to a destination. Stand inside with him for 5 minutes, then unload. Repeat.
5. **Week 5+**: reintroduce short journeys on flat ground, gradually increasing length, never repeating the hill profile that caused the original event until much later.

**Underlying principle**: the original aversion was a Bayesian update. The reset is *also* a series of Bayesian updates. You can't argue with the prior; you can only feed it new evidence in a way it can integrate. Forcing it through the trailer would just generate more aversive evidence and reinforce the wrong association.

**Connection**: the same principle applies to model calibration after a regime break. Once a strategy has been shocked by an out-of-sample event, you can't just keep running it — every drawdown reinforces the bad updates the model is doing. You need to feed it a controlled stream of recoverable conditions until the model's belief about its own competence rebuilds. Backtest the equivalent of the trailer parked in the arena. Then trade small. Then scale.
