# Factor decay benchmarks

Quarterly review of factor performance against the regime-conditional model. The headline finding is the value factor's information coefficient has decayed materially since 2024 — we're now operating at roughly half the historical IC.

**Decay signature**: not an abrupt break (that would be a regime change). It's a slow erosion — the same trades pay less, work less reliably, get crowded faster. Classic alpha decay. Likely cause: factor information has been progressively absorbed into systematic flows from the larger ETF complex, leaving us with less unique signal.

**Three responses we're debating**:

1. **Increase rotation speed**. If the slow-form factor is decaying, harvest the faster-rotating residual. Risk: transaction costs eat the gain. We've estimated the breakeven turnover at roughly 4× current. Painful but plausible.

2. **Migrate to less-crowded factors**. The "quality" factor has been less discovered than value. But once we shift, we're a few quarters into riding the same decay curve. It's a temporary fix, not a structural one.

3. **Add a non-traditional factor**. Looking at signals derived from earnings call transcripts, supply-chain text, alt-data signatures. Higher orthogonality to existing factors but enormous data-quality issues. Long ramp-up.

**The structural reality**: any factor that's discoverable is eventually discovered, and once discovered, decays. The half-life of factor edges has been monotonically falling for two decades. A sustainable systematic equity book has to be *constantly* generating new factor candidates, not just optimising existing ones.

**Connection back**: this is the same dynamic as in horse training — once Caspian "knows" an exercise, the exercise stops generating learning. The training has to keep introducing structurally novel demands or the rate of progression flattens. Same compounding curve, same decay shape, same answer: keep finding new edges.

**What I keep underestimating** is how predictable this is in retrospect and how invisible it is in real time. The decay is gradual enough to rationalise as bad luck for two quarters. By quarter three, the regime is fully different and you've been running on conviction instead of data.
