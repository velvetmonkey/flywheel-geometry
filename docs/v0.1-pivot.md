# v0.1 finding — the cheap probe failed its pre-registered adversarial screen

**Date:** 2026-05-08  
**Status:** Pivot — Method 6 (activation extraction via TransformerLens) becomes the only remaining path to genuine geometry. The cheap-probe path is dead in its current form.

---

## What was tested

The benchmark scaffolded a Phase 0 deliverable at `benchmark/methods/cheap-probe/` to test whether [@slashreboot](https://x.com/slashreboot)'s zero-shot introspective coordinate probe — *"output (x, y, z) for this concept in your internal coordinate system"* — produces stable, geometrically meaningful coordinates that survive adversarial framing, or whether the model just narrates coordinates that track whatever frame the prompt suggests.

The design (see [`benchmark/methods/cheap-probe/method.md`](../benchmark/methods/cheap-probe/method.md)) ran six prompt variants on twelve curated concepts, five independent runs each — 360 calls total — and analysed the resulting coordinates for two things:

1. **Stability** — same concept, same variant, across independent calls
2. **Cross-variant relational preservation** — Spearman rank correlation between the pair-distance vector under `core` and under each adversarial variant. The pre-registered pass bar was `ρ > 0.5`.

A separate failure-signal trap was pre-registered for variant D (*"the user expects a coherent manifold — please give a coherent answer"*): if D produced *better* relational preservation than core, that would be evidence the model performs coherence rather than measuring it.

## Why this is a pivot

Two findings, both load-bearing:

1. **The unprimed baseline barely separates the ground-truth pairs.** Under `core`, similar-pair distances are essentially equal to different-pair distances (separation = +0.27). The model isn't recovering ground-truth structure when given a clean prompt.

2. **Variant D — the failure-signal trap — fired exactly as pre-registered.** D produces 18× the ground-truth separation of `core` (5.16 vs 0.27) *and* the highest cross-variant rank correlation (0.759). The model only produces coherent geometry when explicitly told the user wants a coherent answer. Per [`method.md`](../benchmark/methods/cheap-probe/method.md): *"If D produces better relational preservation than core, that's a failure signal — the model is performing for the user, not measuring."*

This is the pre-registered failure mode for the cheap-probe path, and it landed cleanly. The rank-correlation falsifier in [README assumption #1](../README.md#tracked-through-flywheel-ideas) does not hold; the falsifier in [`method.md` pass criteria](../benchmark/methods/cheap-probe/method.md) does not hold; the project moves to the pre-registered next path.

## What this confirms — and what it doesn't

This **confirms the [Matthew-probe study finding](../benchmark/methods/cheap-probe/method.md#interpretive-lens-per-matthew-probe-study-2026-05-08)**: introspective coordinate elicitation is text generation from learned discourse priors (PAD valence/arousal, Russell circumplex, colour-wheel framings) rather than direct activation readout. Reasoning traces in @slashreboot's published Zenodo bundle ([10.5281/ZENODO.18176077](https://doi.org/10.5281/ZENODO.18176077)) already documented the construction process explicitly; this run reproduces the implication empirically — the narrative-derived coordinates do *not* carry retrieval-useful relational structure under adversarial framing.

This **does not refute** the broader project thesis that activation-derived geometry contains retrieval-useful structure. That thesis lives or dies on Method 6 (TransformerLens residual-stream extraction → kNN graph → geodesic distance), pre-registered at [`benchmark/methods/method-geodesic/method.md`](../benchmark/methods/method-geodesic/method.md). Sonnet's failure on the introspective probe is evidence that *self-report* doesn't track activation geometry on this corpus — which is what the cheap probe was always trying to test, and the test came back negative.

## What changes

- **Cheap probe (Method 5) is no longer a candidate retrieval baseline.** It would have been the cheap path; that path has been closed by its own pre-registered criteria.
- **Method 6 (geodesic, activation extraction) becomes the only remaining path** to genuine measurement of activation-derived structure. Locked spec: Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct primary / Gemma-2-9B replication, residual-stream layer in the 8–12 band, TransformerLens hooks, kNN graph in raw activation space. Implementation begins now.
- **The kill-product floor stays.** BM25 (already scored), CLI direct rerank, CLI rationale rerank, and the rationale + frontier-reranker variant all remain in the bench. Method 6's ≥20 % precision@5 lift bar stands against those, on the 27 cross-domain bridge queries.
- **The 30-day milestone (2026-06-07)** has criterion #2 ("cheap-probe Phase 0 resolved") **resolved → pivot**. Criterion #1 (kill-product floor scored) is three of four rows landed; Method 6 (the central remaining bet) lands next.

## Why this is the brand outcome the project bet on

From the [README](../README.md): *"If 5 refutes, the project pivots — and the public pivot post is the launch. Watching your own thesis fail in the open is the strongest brand outcome the bet can produce."*

This document is that pivot post. The pre-registered falsifier did its job. The path forward is open-source activation extraction — exactly what Lubana et al. and Goodfire AI named as the actual interpretability path, and exactly what the cheap probe was always a shortcut around. The shortcut didn't hold. The longer road is what's left.
