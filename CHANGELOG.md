- **2026-05-08** — Method 6 (geodesic) implementation scaffolded as a two-stage pipeline at `benchmark/methods/method-geodesic/`. Stage 1 (`extract.py`, runs on rented GPU) loads `meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct` via TransformerLens, extracts last-token residual-stream tensors at layers 8 / 10 / 12 to a single `.npz`. Stage 2 (`run.py`, local) reads the `.npz`, builds a kNN graph (k = 10, Euclidean, symmetric union, deterministic tie-break), computes shortest-path geodesic distances, retrieves top-5 per query, emits eval-format JSONL plus `.graphml` graph dumps and per-layer graph diagnostics. Pre-registration block in `method.md` locks layer 10 as primary, layers 8 / 12 as sensitivity, random-kNN at layer 10 as negative control, and two hard gates for `asm-3zmj1VGB` resolution (`≥1.20 × Method 3 p@5` AND `> random-kNN p@5 + 0.05` absolute). Backend stub stays as `extract.py` until the rental session fires; Stage 2 is independently smoke-tested against mock random tensors and passes the eval pre-scoring guards.
- **2026-05-08** — Activation artifact contract written into `method-geodesic/method.md`: model identity + revision SHA, layer indices (TransformerLens hook names), token selection / pooling, normalisation (none in mainline), persistence format (`np.savez_compressed` with `corpus_layer_{N}` / `query_layer_{N}` keys at float32), distance metric (Euclidean → graph shortest-path). Stage 1 and Stage 2 communicate only through this contract; either side that diverges fails the manifest's reproducibility check.
